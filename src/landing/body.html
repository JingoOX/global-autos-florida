What is our
broker service?

Real auction price · No friction

We are your trusted broker.

We hold access to private auctions and the nation's leading vehicle auction platforms, where we help you source the exact vehicle you're after — any make, model and condition.

Our team executes the entire process with you: from vehicle selection and live auction bidding, through transport coordination and full documentation. You make the calls — we execute, protect, and answer for the rest.

How it works

Seven steps, zero friction. You set the rules — we run the entire process with you.

01

Pick your vehicle

Find the vehicle you want on Copart, IAA or Manheim — or let us surface options that match your spec.

02

Set your budget

We help you lock the maximum bid you're willing to place. That becomes your ceiling — we never bid a dollar above it.

03

Place your security deposit

A 10% security deposit is required to participate. If the purchase closes, the deposit is applied to the vehicle price.

04

We place the bid

Our team enters the auction and bids up to your authorized maximum. No surprises, no hidden fees.

05

If we win, you pay

Once the auction is won, the vehicle must be paid within the auction's deadline. Your security deposit counts toward payment.

06

We coordinate transport

We handle the move from the auction yard to wherever you need it — your home, our lot, or anywhere in between.

07

Documentation

We handle title processing and every document tied to the deal, so your vehicle ends up legal, registered and ready to roll.

04 / About">04 / About

Not just a dealer —
a new way to buy

Global Autos Florida LLC was built in the heart of Kissimmee on a simple conviction: buying a car in this country shouldn't feel like a battle — and it shouldn't be fought in a language that isn't yours.

We started with a clean slate and a sharper idea: a tech-driven, fully transparent dealership where the price you see is the price you pay, where damaged credit doesn't close doors, and where every vehicle clears a 150-point inspection before it touches the lot.

We're a modern, owner-operated business — not a chain run by out-of-state investors. That means the person who sells you the car today is the same one picking up the phone six months later, when you come back with a question or to plan the next move.

Beyond pre-owned sales and financing for every credit profile, we help clients buy directly from the major U.S. auctions through our broker service — same principle as always: real access, transparent price, end-to-end execution.
